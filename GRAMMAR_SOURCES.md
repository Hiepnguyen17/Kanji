# Grammar sources and attribution

KanjiAI groups the full Japanese Language Data N5 inventory into a beginner,
Minna-style sequence of 25 lessons. The source patterns, English descriptions,
and source examples retain their CC BY-SA 4.0 provenance. N4 and higher lesson
structure, Vietnamese explanations, and examples are original project content.

## Pattern inventory

The selection and JLPT-level classification of grammar patterns come from or
were checked against [Japanese Language Data](https://github.com/jkindrix/japanese-language-data),
licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. When distributing a direct derivative of that
dataset, retain its attribution and share-alike obligations.

## Learning-path reference

[Nhai Kanji](https://nhaikanji.com/bunpo) was consulted only for its public
information architecture: topic-first lessons, a visible formula, usage notes,
examples, and related practice. No explanatory text, examples, or visual assets
were copied from that site.
